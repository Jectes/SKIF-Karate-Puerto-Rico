The Dojo

A serious place to train, grow, and earn every step.

S.K.I.F. Puerto Rico Honbu Dojo represents a disciplined Shotokan environment where students develop strong fundamentals, self-control, confidence, and respect. The site keeps the dojo identity formal while adding a secure foundation for student and family records.
